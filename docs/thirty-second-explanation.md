# 30-second explanation

Deal teams take expert-call findings and write them up as a short brief. The weak point is the write-up: a figure gets copied wrongly, a segment number becomes "the whole market", or two experts disagree and only one gets cited.

Evidence Review checks each claim against the exact quotes it cites, and against every other quote on the same figure. It separates real contradictions from different cohorts. A brief can't be marked handoff-ready until every claim has a source and every risky one has a human decision. Whatever is still open becomes a neutral, template-based guide for the next call.

It is a one-week, browser-only prototype with synthetic data. It shows the workflow; it doesn't claim customer results.
