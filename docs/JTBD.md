# Jobs to be done

Hypotheses, not interview findings.

## Core job

**When** I have finished the expert calls for a workstream and must send a synthesis to my VP or the IC pack, **I want to** know that every sentence I send is backed by what an expert actually said, for the right segment and period, **so I can** hand off without being caught out by a partner's question or a later contradiction.

## Related jobs

| When… | I want to… | So I can… |
|---|---|---|
| two experts give different numbers | see whether they are talking about the same thing | tell a real conflict from a cohort difference before I escalate it |
| a claim is weak | record why, and keep the history | defend my judgement later |
| gaps remain | get a neutral list of questions for the next call | close gaps without leading the expert |
| I share a brief outside the deal team | remove names and contact details I chose | lower the chance of exposing an expert, while knowing it isn't anonymisation |
| a partner asks "where did this come from?" | click straight to the quote | answer in seconds |

## Forces

- **Push:** embarrassment of a wrong figure in an IC memo; time spent re-reading transcripts.
- **Pull:** one gate that says "ready" for a defined reason.
- **Anxiety:** "another tool that slows me down"; false positives.
- **Habit:** copy quotes into a doc and trust memory.
